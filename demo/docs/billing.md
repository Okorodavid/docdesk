# Billing and payments

## When is my bill due

Your bill is due on the same day each month, shown on the first page of your bill and in the app under Billing. Payment is taken automatically if autopay is on.

## Late payments

If a payment is more than 10 days late, a late fee of 5 dollars is added. Service is paused after 30 days without payment and resumes within one hour after you pay.

## Turn on autopay

Open the Brightline app, go to Billing, then Payment methods, and switch on Autopay. Customers on autopay get 3 dollars off every month.

## Change your plan

You can move to a faster or slower plan at any time in the app under Plan. Upgrades start right away. Downgrades start at your next billing date.
