# Outages and slow internet

## Check for an outage in your area

Open the Brightline app and look at the banner on the Home screen. If there is a known outage in your area, the banner shows it with an estimated fix time. You can also text OUTAGE to 32211.

## What to do when the internet is slow

First restart your router. Then move the router to an open, central spot away from microwaves and thick walls. Devices on the 5 GHz network are faster but have shorter range than the 2.4 GHz network.

If speeds are still below half of your plan speed after a restart, run the speed test in the Brightline app and contact support with the result.

## Outage credits

If your service is down for more than 24 hours in a row because of a Brightline outage, you get a credit for each full day you were offline. The credit appears automatically on your next bill. You do not need to ask for it.
