# Router setup and restarts

## Restart your router

To restart your Brightline router, unplug the power cable, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. The lights take about 3 minutes to turn solid white, which means you are back online.

You can also restart it from the Brightline app: open Devices, choose your router, and tap Restart.

## Reset your router to factory settings

A factory reset erases your Wi-Fi name and password. Press and hold the reset button on the back of the router for 15 seconds with a paperclip, until the light blinks orange. Setup starts again after about 5 minutes.

Only reset if a restart did not fix the problem.

## Change your Wi-Fi password

Open the Brightline app, go to Wi-Fi, and tap Change password. Every connected device has to rejoin with the new password.

## Router lights

Solid white means connected. Blinking white means the router is starting up. Solid orange means there is no internet signal reaching your home. Blinking orange means the router is resetting.
