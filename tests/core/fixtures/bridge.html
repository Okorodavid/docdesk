Bridge FAQ

The Example bridge moves ETH and USDC between Ethereum mainnet and Example Chain.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawals from Example Chain to Ethereum take 7 days because of the fraud-proof challenge window.

Deposits from Ethereum to Example Chain usually arrive in about 15 minutes.

My bridge transaction is stuck pending

  • Check the transaction hash on the Example Chain explorer.
  • If it shows Ready to prove, click Prove in the bridge app.
bridge status --tx 0xabc123

Bridge fees

There is no bridge fee. You only pay network gas on each side.
